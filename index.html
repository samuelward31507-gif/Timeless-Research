Frequently Asked Questions

How are capsule amounts listed?+
The listed amount is per capsule, not the total package. Every pack contains 30 capsules. Total compound = listed amount multiplied by 30.
How does the group split work?+
Each research partner pays 50% of the listed price. Simply mention when placing your order and we will arrange accordingly.
How long does shipping take?+
4 to 14 business days estimated. Tracking is provided once shipped. All orders are dispatched in discreet, unmarked packaging with no indication of contents.
Are products third-party lab tested?+
Yes. Every compound is independently tested for purity and identity. Certificates of Analysis are available on request for any product and lot number.
How do I reconstitute lyophilized peptide vials?+
Use Sterile Water (ID: WA10) for immediate use, or Bacteriostatic Water (ID: BAC10) to extend shelf life after reconstitution. BAC water is recommended for ongoing research use.
When does free shipping apply?+
Free shipping is applied automatically to any order totaling $300 or more. No discount code required.
What compounds are coming soon?+
