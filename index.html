01 — WHAT ARE PEPTIDES

Chains of Amino Acids With Defined Research Utility

Peptides are short chains of amino acids — the building blocks of proteins — that play signaling roles throughout biological systems. In the research context, synthetic peptides allow scientists to study these signaling pathways in controlled laboratory environments.

02 — ANALYTICAL TESTING

HPLC Purity Analysis and Mass Spectrometry

Every production lot is analyzed using High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) to verify purity, and Mass Spectrometry (MS) to confirm molecular identity. These two analytical methods together establish that a compound is both pure and correctly identified.

03 — STORAGE & HANDLING

Lyophilization for Long-Term Research Stability

Lyophilized (freeze-dried) peptides provide superior stability compared to liquid formulations. Stored at the correct temperature and protected from moisture and UV light, lyophilized compounds retain their integrity throughout extended research programs.

04 — DOCUMENTATION

Research Reproducibility Depends on Material Consistency

Batch documentation — including purity data, molecular weight confirmation, and lot numbers — allows researchers to account for material variables in their protocols and replicate results accurately across studies.

Quality & Testing

Our Standard
Process.

Every compound follows the same five-stage process before it reaches a researcher's hands.

01
Source Material

Pharmaceutical-Grade Raw Inputs

We source amino acid inputs and synthesis precursors from validated pharmaceutical-grade suppliers. Incoming materials are evaluated for certificate compliance before entering our production process.

02
Synthesis

Solid-Phase Peptide Synthesis

Peptides are assembled using Solid-Phase Peptide Synthesis (SPPS), a method that provides precise control over amino acid sequence and allows for intermediate purification steps throughout the synthesis process.

03
Analytical Testing

HPLC Purity and Mass Spectrometry

Every batch undergoes HPLC analysis to quantify purity (≥98% threshold required for release) and mass spectrometry for molecular identity confirmation. Results are documented and retained per lot.

04
Batch Verification

Independent Review Before Release

Test results are reviewed against compound-specific specifications. Batches failing purity thresholds are rejected and not released. Lot numbers are assigned only to batches meeting all specifications.

05
Documentation

Certificate of Analysis — Every Lot

A Certificate of Analysis is issued for each production lot, containing purity data, molecular weight, CAS number, lot identifier, and test date. COAs are available on request for any product in our catalog.
