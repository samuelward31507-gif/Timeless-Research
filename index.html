Quality & testing

Our standard process.

Every compound follows the same five stages before it reaches a researcher's bench.

STAGE 01

Source material

Amino acid inputs sourced from validated pharmaceutical-grade suppliers with certificate compliance checks before production.

STAGE 02

Synthesis

Solid-phase peptide synthesis gives precise control over sequence, with intermediate purification throughout the run.

STAGE 03

Analytical testing

Every batch undergoes HPLC purity analysis at a ≥98% threshold and mass spectrometry for molecular identity.

STAGE 04

Batch verification

Independent review before release. Batches failing purity thresholds are rejected; lot numbers are issued only on a pass.

STAGE 05

Documentation

A Certificate of Analysis is issued per lot with purity data, molecular weight, CAS number, lot ID and test date.
