01 — WHAT ARE PEPTIDES

Chains of Amino Acids With Defined Research Utility

Peptides are short chains of amino acids that play signaling roles throughout biological systems. Synthetic peptides allow scientists to study these pathways in controlled laboratory environments.

02 — ANALYTICAL TESTING

HPLC Purity Analysis and Mass Spectrometry

Every production lot is analyzed using HPLC to verify purity and mass spectrometry to confirm molecular identity. Both methods together establish that a compound is pure and correctly identified.

03 — STORAGE & HANDLING

Lyophilization for Long-Term Research Stability

Lyophilized peptides provide superior stability. Stored correctly, compounds retain integrity throughout extended research programs.

04 — DOCUMENTATION

Research Reproducibility Depends on Material Consistency

Batch documentation allows researchers to account for material variables and replicate results accurately across studies.
