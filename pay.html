Accounts & payment

Paying for an order.

Orders are invoiced once your account is verified and you have accepted a quotation. There is no checkout on this site, and nothing ships against an unverified account.

  1. Request

    Build a request list from the catalogue and send it with your account application. Prices on the catalogue are list prices; they exclude shipping and tax.

  2. Verification

    We confirm the institution, the research facility address, the responsible investigator, an institutional email address and the intended research use. Nothing is invoiced before this is complete.

  3. Quotation

    We issue a written quotation against your request list, confirming lot availability, any quantity break, shipping method and the total including tax and carriage. It is valid for 30 days.

  4. Invoice

    On acceptance we raise an invoice through Stripe. It arrives by email from accounts@timelessresearch.com and carries a secure payment page hosted by Stripe, where you can pay by card or bank transfer. We never see or store your card details.

  5. Release

    Material is released once payment clears, unless written credit terms have been agreed in advance. The certificate of analysis for the supplied lot travels with the shipment.

How to know an invoice is really ours

Our invoices arrive only from accounts@timelessresearch.com, and the payment page they link to is hosted by Stripe on a stripe.com address. We will never telephone you for card details, never ask you to pay a different account from the one on the invoice, and never send payment instructions that change bank details at short notice. If anything about a payment request looks wrong, stop and contact us on the address above before paying.

Questions we are asked

Can I pay by card immediately?

Not before the account is verified. Once it is, the Stripe invoice takes a card and settles straight away, so in practice payment is instant from that point on.

Do you take purchase orders?

Yes, from verified institutions. Send the PO reference with your acceptance of the quotation and it is carried on the invoice. Credit terms have to be agreed in writing first; otherwise payment is due in advance.

What currency?

All prices and invoices are in USD. Your bank may apply its own conversion and charges.

Can you ship before payment clears?

Only against agreed credit terms. See terms of sale, section 4.
