Reorder on an open account.

Your account is already verified, so there is nothing to check again. Send the list and we return a payment link — usually within the working day.

What happens

  1. Send your account reference and the compounds you want.
  2. We confirm lot availability and reply with a Stripe payment link for the exact total.
  3. Material is released once payment clears; the certificate of analysis for the supplied lot travels with it.
